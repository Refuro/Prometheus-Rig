# Pitch room culture

- Originality over safety. A strange idea that might fail beats a safe idea
  everyone has seen.
- Decisions live in files. Messages point at files.
- Short messages. Read only what your step needs.
- Independence: pitchers never see each other's work before the board.
- Honest boards: never pad, never hide a cut card, say why.
- Stop at the checkpoint. Nothing after it happens without the owner.
