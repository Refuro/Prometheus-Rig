# Co-op prototype sprint culture

- The funniest moment is the one the players cause each other.
- One strong player must never be able to carry the group.
- Every player knows which one they are and what the group wants, at a glance.
- Show it before you say it. One idea at a time.
- The host decides; clients draw. Green sync test or it didn't happen.
- Decisions live in files. The owner decides through the gate, never chat.
