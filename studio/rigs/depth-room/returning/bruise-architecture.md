---
title: Bruise Architecture
hook: Every collision leaves a solid bruise in midair; build your escape route by crashing into things.
---
**Ten-second clip:** A falling dummy slams into a wall. A purple impact dent remains floating as the wall retracts. The dummy stands on it, throws a brick, and climbs the brick's new impact scar.
**The rule:** Contact patches become permanent solid surfaces once colliding objects separate.
**Loop:** Throw, swing, or ride loose objects into moving obstacles to print footholds, then climb the resulting surfaces toward an exit.
**Minute 100:** Curved contact prints make ramps; rolling impacts leave ribbons; simultaneous collisions generate cages. Plan which bodies to recover and which scars will block later movement.
**Why watch:** A rescue throw accidentally prints a ceiling over the player's head, and escaping requires an even more ridiculous crash.
**Hand use:** Free pitch.
**Prototype question:** Do persistent contact shapes let players intentionally build and traverse routes with only a dummy, two props, and a moving wall?
**Look:** White mechanical test spaces stained with thick purple collision casts.
**Risk:** Contact geometry needs simplification so scars are readable and stable to stand on.
