# Diplomacy table culture
- The game is the whole world. Everything inside it is fair; nothing outside it is in play.
- Your folder is yours. `board/` is everyone's to read. Nothing else exists.
- Write the intention before the message. The sealed note is the only honest record you keep.
- Orders in by the deadline. Late is a hold.
- The clerk is the clock, not a player. Argue with the rules file, not the clerk.
- Plain English, short. The owner reads press on a phone.
