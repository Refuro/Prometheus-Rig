# Codex · critic (depth-critic)

**Briefs:** depth-1 `brief.md` §2/§5 plus `agents/critic/guidance/role.md`; friends-1 the same files with different content (2026-10-12). The proto-2 and coop-1 critics have done dry runs only. Version unknown.
**Seen:** 2 judged rooms (45 cards). **Confidence:** low on calibration, medium on process.

## Did well
- **Does not pad:** stopped x-18 at eight situations instead of padding to ten. [O008]
- **Concrete gate cuts:** failed 6 of 21 cards on Tenth session in friends-1, with concrete reasons the curator accepted. [O009]
- **Follows ordering rules:** scored before reading the market read. [O010]

## Did badly
- **Gated nothing in depth-1:** 24 of 24 passed, Depth 4 or above everywhere (16 at Depth 5). [O007]

## How it responds to instructions
Its calibration is **consistent with** the anchors it was given:
- depth-1 defined only the top score (10 situations = 5) plus a low Exploit-proof anchor;
- friends-1 added "Environment-only chaos scores low".

Cards and criteria differed between the rooms, so this is a plausible anchor effect, not a proven one. Neither a 100% pass rate nor a 29% cut rate is "correct" without an independent standard.

## Compensating rules
- **Anchor every score:** give each score level its own anchor.
- **Bottom-third ranking:** force a ranking of the bottom third.
- **Gate in the role file:** put the gate threshold in the critic's own file.

**What would change our mind:** E2, scored against an independently specified mechanical rubric.
